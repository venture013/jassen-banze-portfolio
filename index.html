04About

I’m Donell — referred to as Jassen.

I lead creative work across a product studio, a student social network and The Intellect Few.

My eye for design comes from architecture. I discovered it by crafting house models out of cardboard and, without attending art school, taught myself with what I had — using Planner5D as my mobile design tool.

Today that same eye goes into brands: the visuals people see, the words they read and the plan that decides what goes out next. My goal is to keep learning, and to recreate and innovate existing ideas.
